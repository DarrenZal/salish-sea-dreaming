## Prompt (paste verbatim)

```
Context: Salish Sea Dreaming Phase 2, HR MacMillan Space Centre, late May 2026.
Three 1080p projectors in a transit anteroom. Austin Harry's creative vision:
Thunderbird passes down a pearl containing three Coast Salish design shapes
swirling/morphing inside; viewers feel they are inside the pearl. May version
is a 3-panel triptych; strategic arc is full-dome interactive version at
MOVE37XR Oct / Life at Center 2027.

Research only this:

1. Visual precedents for "being inside an object" or "inside a vessel /
   sphere / pearl / heart / cell" across non-contiguous panels, triptychs,
   altarpieces, immersive AV, projection art, CAVE, or fulldome work.

2. Compositional strategies that make three discrete screens feel like
   windows into one continuous interior volume without edge blending.

3. Triptych-to-dome migration patterns: how to author flat multi-panel
   assets now so they can later map to fulldome or 360 / CAVE without
   repainting everything.

4. Pearl / sphere / vessel containment motifs in contemporary projection
   / digital art, especially where the motif is culturally named by a
   collaborator (not appropriated as a visual device).

5. Risks: when the "inside object" metaphor becomes visually confusing,
   decorative, or culturally overdetermined.

Deliverable:
- 10–15 concrete precedents, each with source link, image / video reference
  if available, and why it matters.
- A practical May 2026 composition recipe for 3 × 1080p triptych.
- A migration recipe for fulldome.
- Clear separation between sourced precedent and researcher inference.
- No generic immersive-art survey.
```
