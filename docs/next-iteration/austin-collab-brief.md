## 1. Why him, why now

Prav has booked a meeting with Austin to integrate the SSD dreaming system with Austin's work on a "space center projection wall" (per 2026-04-14 meeting notes). Austin is one of very few Coast Salish artists already fluent in real-time 3D pipelines, and his **Salish Spirit at VanLive!** piece — a 3D Thunderbird composited live over Vancouver footage on a public LED screen — is the closest one-line cousin to SSD's "Salish Sea using technology to perceive itself" thesis. He framed it as **"screens as windows into the supernatural"**[^1] — lead the pitch with that bridge.

## 2. Identity & lineage

- **Name:** Austin Aan'yas Harry. Studio: INDIGITAL, Vancouver, BC.[^2]
- **Cultural lineage:** Wolf Clan, Sḵwx̱wú7mesh (Squamish Nation); Thunderbird Clan, Nam̓gis Nation (Kwakwaka'wakw, northern Vancouver Island).[^2]
- **Family:** son of master Coast Salish carver **Xwalacktun (Rick Harry, O.B.C.)**, lifetime achievement award recipient[^3][^4]; brother of sculptor **James Harry**.[^5] Three exhibited together as *Balanced Forms* at the West Vancouver Art Museum.[^6]
- **Contact:** austin@indigitaldesign.ca · (778) 994-9464 · M–F 10am–8pm.[^2]

## 3. Practice

Mission (his words): *"Bring traditional Coast Salish heritage and culture to a modern audience through digital media."*[^2] Tagline: *"Modern design anchored in ancient culture."*

- **Tools:** Autodesk Maya, ZBrush, Substance Painter, 3D printing.[^2]
- **Background:** video-game industry before founding INDIGITAL.[^2]
- **Bridging concept** (from his Kalkalilh page): bridges digital art and sculpture, character design and Coast Salish design, supernatural and natural worlds.[^7]

## 4. Closest precedent for SSD — Salish Spirit at VanLive!

City of Vancouver Public Art Board commission, *Platforms: Nine Places for Seeing*. Co-presented with Jonas Jones; Robson/Granville LED screen; Jan 1 – Feb 26 2024; **10-month production**.[^1][^8] 3D Thunderbird composited over live Vancouver footage. Closest formal precedent in his portfolio for what SSD is doing technically; biggest tonal/conceptual cousin too.

## 6. Style overlap with the SSD species library

Austin's existing crest repertoire already includes **Orca, Salmon, Eagle, Bear, Hummingbird** (Westridge mural + VGH installations) — direct overlap with SSD's iNaturalist species set.

**Concept hook for the call:** his crests as a togglable **cultural-lens overlay** on the iNat species deck — Western-science image plus Coast Salish formline rendition of the same animal — directly maps to SSD's *three-eyed seeing* thesis (Western science + Indigenous knowledge + the land itself). This is a non-trivial pitch — it makes Austin a co-author of the visual grammar, not a service provider.

## 7. Cultural protocol — questions to raise on the call

Do **not** assume on these. The brief is honest about what we don't know yet.

1. **Which motifs are open vs ceremonial?** Sínulhka is already public via the YVR install; Thunderbird is his clan crest. What's the protocol for AI-generated derivatives?
2. **AI training acceptability?** Briony Penn agreed to a LoRA fine-tune; cultural protocol may rule that out for Austin entirely. Default our planning to img2img / IP-Adapter / direct compositing rather than fine-tune unless he says otherwise.
3. **Attribution model.** Marvel / Kabam precedent: cultural-centre partnership with **Squamish Lil'wat Cultural Centre**. Should we mirror that here?
4. **Halact / Squamish elders.** Prav named Halact and son as Squamish-territory collaborators for MOVE37XR (Oct). Does Austin want a joint conversation or sequential intros?
5. **Family network.** Interest in looping Xwalacktun or James Harry into Phase 2 venues for tactile/sculptural counterpoint to the projection layer?

## 8. Family / network multiplier

Engaging Austin opens doors to **Xwalacktun** (Squamish elder, master carver) and **James Harry** (sculptor) — relevant if any Phase 2 venue wants a tactile/sculptural counterpoint to the screen-based work. *Balanced Forms* establishes the three of them together in a fine-art context, not just commercial design.[^6]

## 9. Watch-outs

- **Tooling delta.** No public mention of TouchDesigner / Unreal / Unity / AR. Game-industry background suggests fast onboarding, but assume first-time tooling for our stack.
- **Capacity.** Solo studio, M–F 10–8.[^2] Don't load him with multi-track work without explicit time-boxing.
- **Pricing.** No public rate sheet. Ask early.
- **Prior art ≠ permission.** A motif appearing on his public portfolio (e.g. Sínulhka at YVR) does not imply blanket reuse rights. Ask per-motif.

## 10. Pitch hooks (for Prav)

- **Conceptual bridge:** Salish Spirit / VanLive! → SSD. "Screens as windows into the supernatural" ↔ "the Salish Sea using technology to perceive itself."
- **Indigenomics fit:** Austin's MST Justice Centre work was already under Sḵwx̱wú7mesh + Musqueam + Tsleil-Waututh shared-territory protocol — exactly the framing Carol Anne is curating.
- **MOVE37XR (Oct, Squamish territory):** literally his territory. Natural anchor venue for a multi-month build.
- **Three-eyed seeing as visual grammar:** his crests + iNat species + Briony watercolors as three coherent layers, not three competing aesthetics.
