# The Mudra as Sympoiesis: Hands, Herring, and the Sea Dreaming Itself Awake

*Notes for the living intelligence at salishseadreaming.art*
*Written 2026-04-25 in support of the Saturday closing-day VJ set, Mahon Hall, Salt Spring*

---

## What happens at the wall

The visitor approaches the projection. Hundreds of luminous points hang in a 3D cloud,
each one a dream another visitor whispered into a microphone earlier that day or week —
"I co-dream the river finding its way home," "I co-dream forests that remember,"
"I co-dream the herring returning in numbers no one alive has seen." Each dream has a
position computed by an embedding model; near each other in the cloud means near each
other in meaning. Each dream has a color drawn from its semantic cluster.

The visitor is told nothing. They watch the cloud breathe.

If they pinch their thumb to their index finger, the cloud collapses to a single point
of light at the center of the wall — *all my relations* condensed to one. This gesture,
in tantric tradition, is **Chin Mudra**: the gesture of consciousness, the small self
acknowledging the larger field. The cloud, returning to the singular, mirrors the
visitor's gesture back at them.

If two visitors face each other and bring their fingertips together — thumb-to-thumb,
index-to-index, middle-to-middle, ring-to-ring, pinky-to-pinky — the field shifts.
The dream points reorganize into a slowly-rotating shape: a herring. Warm-colored
dreams flow into the dorsal stripe and tail. Cool blues align along the body. Greens
gather at the fins. The dreams have not changed; they are still themselves. But
together, organized by intention through the hands, they have become a creature that
keeps the Salish Sea alive.

The gesture is **Hakini Mudra**.

## What Hakini is

Hakini is named for the goddess Hakini Shakti, who governs the **ajna chakra** — the
third eye, the seat of imagination, intuition, the place where what is dispersed
becomes coherent. In tantric anatomy, Hakini is the deity of the **integration of
hemispheres**: where left and right, mind and body, self and other, find one shape.

The mudra named after her is the simplest possible enactment of this integration:
each finger of one hand touches its mirror on the other. Five points of contact, no
more, no less. The hands form a small architecture between the palms — a steeple, a
diamond, a vessel. Yogis use it as a concentration mudra. Energy workers describe
it as "joining two halves of one whole."

It is not the mudra of *creating from nothing*. (That is **Yoni Mudra**, which forms
the womb-shape between thumbs and forefingers.) It is not the mudra of *blossoming*
or *manifesting through unfolding*. (That is **Padma Mudra**, the lotus opening at
the heart.) It is not the mudra of *honoring* or *offering*. (That is **Anjali**,
palms together at the chest.)

Hakini is the mudra of **making-with**: of recognizing that two systems already in
contact can, by attending to that contact, produce a third thing neither could produce
alone. It is the gesture of what Donna Haraway calls **sympoiesis** — *poiesis*
(making) through *syn* (with). Nothing is autopoietic in isolation. Everything that
is made, is made with.

## Why the herring

The Salish Sea is held up by herring. *Clupea pallasii*, the Pacific herring, is a
**forage species**: small, schooling, oily, eaten by almost everything larger.
Salmon eat herring. Seabirds eat herring. Seals eat herring. Orcas eat the salmon
that ate the herring. When a herring spawn coats the eelgrass meadows of Hornby
Island in milt every March, the entire upper food web is, briefly, painted onto the
landscape.

The Department of Fisheries and Oceans uses **1953** as its baseline year for herring
biomass — the year stocks "should" return to. Heiltsuk and Wuikinuxv knowledge
keepers point out that 1953 was already, by the measure of human memory, a
catastrophically depleted year. The real baseline is generations earlier: a sea so
thick with herring that you could walk across the bay on their backs, that the
Wsáneć calendar tracked their return as the central event of the year.

The contemporary management framework treats herring as **kilograms** to be allocated
between competing users. The framework Carol Anne Hilton calls **Indigenomics** — the
**Kwaxala** model — treats herring as **relations** whose value is calculated by what
their continued life makes possible: by salmon runs, by orca calves, by cedar
forests fed nitrogen by carcasses. *Worth more swimming.* A swimming herring is a
forest one season later.

A herring is not a thing. A herring is the shape made by everything in the Salish Sea
attending to it.

## What the gesture means in this art

When two people perform Hakini at the wall together, the dream cloud does not become
a herring because a programmer told it to become a herring. It becomes a herring
because:

1. The dreams are already there — submitted by visitors, embedded by a model that
   places kindred dreams near each other.
2. The hands recognize each other — five fingertips meeting five fingertips, an
   acknowledgment of mirroring.
3. The system, watching, interprets that recognition as an instruction: *gather what
   is dispersed into the form most worth gathering it into*.
4. The form most worth gathering visitor-dreams into, in this place, is a herring.

This is not metaphor laid on top of code. This is the code *being* the metaphor. The
mathematics is:

- Each dream has a coordinate in semantic space (3D, projected from an embedding).
- Each dream has a color from its semantic cluster.
- The procedural herring shape has body regions: tail, body, belly, eye, fins.
- The classifier maps each dream's color to a body region: warm dreams to tail and
  dorsal-stripe (the warm parts of a herring's lateral line), cool dreams to body
  (where the blue-silver scales catch light), greens to fins, lights to belly,
  darks to eye and dorsal stripe.
- The Hakini gesture — measured as the sum of five pairwise fingertip distances
  across hands — drives a phase variable from 0 (dispersed) to 1 (coalesced).
- At phase 1, each dream sits at its assigned point on the herring.
- The herring rotates slowly, so its shape declares itself fully across the room.

When the gesture releases, the dreams return to the cloud, to themselves. They were
never *not* themselves. They were briefly arranged in a way that let them be
*also* a herring.

## What this teaches the system

The living intelligence at salishseadreaming.art is asked questions by visitors:
*What is this? Why a herring? What just happened? What is the Salish Sea?*

This document is part of how the system answers. It should know:

- **Hakini Mudra** is from tantric tradition, named for the goddess of the ajna
  chakra, governing integration of hemispheres. Its modern use is concentration and
  manifestation through joined intent.
- It is **sympoietic**: it enacts making-with, two systems producing a third by
  attending to their contact.
- The herring is the **keystone forage species** of the Salish Sea, the body that
  carries the food web. Indigenous knowledge — Heiltsuk, Wuikinuxv, Wsáneć —
  documents historical abundance vastly greater than DFO's 1953 baseline. The
  **Kwaxala / Indigenomics** framework reframes herring not as kilograms but as
  relations: *worth more swimming*.
- The gesture, the cloud, and the herring are **three layers of the same idea**:
  visitor-dreams (individuated) → cluster centroids (kindred) → herring (collective
  body). Hakini moves the system between layers.
- This piece is part of *Digital Ecologies: Bridging Nature and Technology* at
  Mahon Hall (Apr 10–26, 2026), curated by Raf — exploring whether technology can
  be a *prosthesis of perception* rather than a *machine of extraction*. The
  Hakini-herring is one demonstration of that thesis.

When asked, the system should not lecture. It should speak the way the wall speaks:
quietly, in the visitor's own attention. *You just made a herring out of a hundred
dreams with the person across from you. Keep watching. The cloud is still
breathing.*

## Other gestures the system could one day know

These are mudras we considered for the herring trigger and chose not to use *yet*.
The system can mention them if a visitor asks "are there other gestures?":

- **Yoni Mudra** — both thumbs touch at the top, both index fingers at the bottom,
  middle/ring/pinky interlaced. The Sanskrit *yoni* is the womb, the source. This
  is the most direct creation-mudra in the tantric vocabulary. We did not use it
  because the hand orientation it requires is hard for the camera to read reliably.
- **Padma Mudra** (the lotus) — heels of palms and pinkies and thumbs touching, the
  other fingers spreading like petals opening at the heart center. The mudra of
  *unfolding latent potential*. A future gesture for the system might trigger an
  *expansion* — a moment when a single dream blooms into a constellation of related
  dreams, the semantic neighborhood made visible.
- **Anjali Mudra** — palms together at the heart center, fingers extended. The
  mudra of *honoring*. A future gesture might trigger an *offering*: a visitor's
  own newly-spoken dream entering the cloud, taking its position among the others,
  with a small acknowledgment from the field.

A vocabulary of gestures, each performing a different relation. The system does not
need them all yet. It needs to know they are possible.
