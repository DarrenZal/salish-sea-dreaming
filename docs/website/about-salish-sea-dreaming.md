## About Salish Sea Dreaming

*We are the Salish Sea, dreaming itself awake.*

Salish Sea Dreaming is an immersive, real-time AI art installation in which a living bioregion, the Salish Sea, uses technology to perceive itself. You step out of the daylight into a darkened, quiet space, and a large projection surrounds you: herring, salmon, and orca moving through a living watercolor world, generated live and never repeating, grounded in the sea's own ecological data of tide, lunar phase, and river flow. The room fills with sound, an evolving musical score composed from seventy-five years of Pacific herring records, so you hear the history of these waters as music.

At its heart is co-dreaming: holding the sea's beauty and its grief together at once. The work rewards witnessing rather than performance. There is nothing to win and no screen to swipe; the longer you stay, the more you settle into simply being with the sea. Where the space allows, you are invited to co-dream. On your own phone, a gesture and a few words offered to the water, and your dream joins a living archive of everyone's hopes and griefs for the Salish Sea, shared only with your consent.

The work refuses both easy despair and false hope. It holds them together, and opens a door toward the restoration already underway in our own watersheds.
