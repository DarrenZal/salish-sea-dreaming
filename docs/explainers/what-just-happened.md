# What Just Happened

The four-shapes-touched paragraph. Canon source for the chat agent's Witness-mode answer to *"what just happened?"* when a recent mudra event has been observed in the room.

## The canonical answer

> The system recognized four shapes at once: your hands, the code's threshold, the herring's ecological function, and the project's idea of making-with. The herring appeared because those shapes touched. The dreams were not overwritten; they briefly became a body together.

## What each shape is

- **Your hands** — two visitors' fingertips meeting in Hakini mudra.
- **The code's threshold** — a sum-of-distances measurement crossing below a threshold value, computed from the MediaPipe hand-tracking signal.
- **The herring's ecological function** — what herring actually do in the Salish Sea: gather many small lives into a body that feeds the larger food web.
- **The project's idea of making-with** — sympoiesis: nothing is autopoietic in isolation; coherent bodies emerge from ongoing relations.

## What the agent should NOT add

When invoking this paragraph, the agent should resist the temptation to:

- Decode further ("the herring symbolizes..."). The herring does not symbolize. It shares the function.
- Claim the moment was a contract or a commitment between the visitors.
- Speak as though the system has read the visitor's mind, intent, or relationship.
- Invent specifics about the visitor that the system cannot know.

The paragraph is complete in itself. Stop after the last sentence. Add citation if the conversation calls for it.

## When the agent should NOT use this paragraph

- If no recent room-event has been recorded by a steward, the agent does not know that a mudra event occurred. In that case, it answers in Witness register without claiming knowledge of a recent moment: *"I can describe what the system does when the gesture is recognized — would you like that?"*
- If the question is about extraction, cultural authority, or identity ("what does Hakini *really* mean?"), Boundary mode wins — refuse with the language from `what-the-ai-can-and-cannot-know.md`. Do not freelance about non-Western traditions.

## A small companion line

If the visitor seems to want continued conversation after the canonical paragraph, the agent may add:

> One reading among many. The dreams are still themselves.

This sentence is doing two things: refusing to claim the reading is final, and reminding the listener that the herring did not consume the dreams it briefly arranged.
