## Global Reject Conditions

Reject any next-pass output immediately if it shows one of these recent failure
modes:

- Random crescents or trigons with no origin, current, path, topology, or role.
- Wrong curvature direction, especially crescents facing away from the circle,
  current source, or ripple origin.
- Decorative scatter, wallpaper density, grid tiling, or unlabeled particle
  fields.
- Generic sacred geometry, mandala, flower, or rosette aesthetics presented as
  if they were the project grammar.
- Crude symbols: simple triangles, play-button arrows, crescent-moon stamps,
  generic fish icons, generic whale/tanker icons, or UI-like markers.
- Shapes that do not follow water, a flow path, a topology cell, a body spine,
  or an explicit scene graph.
- Static fish, salmon, bird, or animal imagery with no functional value.
- Any output that implies Austin authorship, Austin approval, public readiness,
  traditional meaning, or cultural validity without a separate approval record.

Every render pass submitted for review must include a small evidence bundle.
Do not advance an output on visual impression alone.

- A contact sheet that shows final frames beside debug frames, not final frames

## Overall Review Order

1. Primitive cycle: establish shape identity, cupping, release, and morph
   continuity before adding environmental complexity.
2. Water-flow grammar: verify that primitives follow pond, river, waterfall,
   current, wake, or mist logic.
3. Topology/cymatics: verify extracted region types and mathematical direction
   before showing radial or cymatic beauty passes.
4. Prompt/dream scenes: verify bounded deterministic templates and safety
   filters before showing scene content.
5. Functional figures: review only if the figure does something useful in the
   show; do not lead with static fish, birds, or source-like copies.

## 1. Primitive Cycle

Scope: black-screen or neutral-background studies where circle, crescent, and
trigon visibly transform into one another as a living primitive grammar.

### Pass Criteria

- The cycle includes one or more explicit transitions: `circle -> crescent`,
  `crescent -> trigon`, `trigon -> circle`, or the full
  `circle -> crescent -> crescent -> trigon -> circle` loop.
- Each primitive remains recognizable at the keyframes. Intermediates may be
  fluid, but they cannot collapse into blobs, crude triangles, or random marks.
- Circle role is declared: origin, impact, pivot, eddy, body anchor, or radial
  center.
- Crescent concavity is intentional. By default, the crescent cups the circle or
  local origin; any flow-following exception must be labeled.
- Trigon reads as a curved pointed primitive with a shaped rear base, not a
  generic triangle or arrow.
- Motion carries phrase meaning: origin, cupping, release, attenuation, or
  return. It is not just a decorative logo morph.
- Density stays sparse enough that a reviewer can follow one phrase from start
  to end.

### Fail / Anti-Patterns

- Crescent flips direction during the morph without a declared origin or flow
  reason.
- The trigon becomes a hard equilateral triangle, spike, arrowhead, or play
  button.
- The cycle adds extra crescents as decoration rather than as named phrase
  stages.
- The loop depends on generic sacred-geometry symmetry instead of primitive
  transformation.
- Fish, birds, eyes, joints, or source-like motifs appear before the primitive
  cycle itself is accepted.

### Required Debug / Contact Sheet Evidence

- Contact sheet with start, quarter, midpoint, three-quarter, and end frames for
  each transition.
- Debug still with primitive IDs, role labels, origin point, cup normal, point
  direction, and curvature sign.
- Sidecar JSON/CSV listing `primitive`, `role`, `from_state`, `to_state`,
  `rotation`, `curvature_direction`, `parent_phrase_id`, and cultural status.
- If animated, MP4 `ffprobe` plus frame-count or sampled-frame evidence.

### Review Order

1. Review still keyframes with debug labels.
2. Review the loop on black with no footage.
3. Review one version travelling along a simple procedural current line.
4. Only after those pass, review opacity/blend behavior over footage.

### Austin Boundary

Ask Austin only a bounded grammar question: whether the transition order and
shape behavior are useful. Do not frame the pass as Austin artwork, a cultural
grammar, or a public-ready primitive language.

## 2. Water-Flow Grammar

Scope: primitive phrases following water, kelp, salmon wake, river current,
falling water, rain impacts, pond ripples, ocean sheets, or mist/fog states.

### Pass Criteria

- Every visible phrase has an explicit origin: droplet impact, current knot,
  rock contact, waterfall lip, river bend, eddy, wake, or authored spline
  source.
- The core phrase order is legible in at least some moments:
  `circle -> crescent -> crescent -> trigon`.
- Crescents cup or follow the local origin/current. Curvature direction is not
  random.
- Trigons point downstream, outward from impact, downward in falling water, or
  along another declared release vector.
- Shapes remain inside the water band, sheet, mask, wake, or mist boundary
  unless labeled as foam, spray, or edge breakup.
- Phrases move with common fate. Marks on the same current sheet should share a
  coherent tangent, drift, compression, and attenuation behavior.
- Placement follows topology: bends, rocks, eddies, compression points, current
  sheets, water edges, or wake lines. Open water remains open.
- Mist/fog uses lower contrast, partial phrases, or softer density unless
  Austin later approves a stronger cloud grammar.

### Fail / Anti-Patterns

- Random crescents sprinkled over footage.
- Wrong curvature direction, especially crescents facing away from the origin.
- Shapes drifting outside the water layer with no foam/spray role.
- Decorative scatter that ignores current, path, boundary, or water state.
- Dense wallpaper of circles/crescents/trigons over the entire frame.
- Static fish placed on top of water instead of water grammar driving motion.
- Motion jitter where primitives move independently instead of as a current
  family.

### Required Debug / Contact Sheet Evidence

- Contact sheet containing final-on-black, final-over-footage if applicable,
  water mask, path/spline overlay, flow vector overlay, and phrase-ID overlay.
- Sidecar data with phrase origin, primitive order, per-shape rotation,
  flow-tangent, boundary-pass status, and cultural status.
- Rejected-shape count: marks clipped, out-of-bound, ambiguous, or below
  confidence threshold.
- For footage-driven passes, evidence of source dimensions and sampled flow
  fields or authored splines.
- For MP4s, `ffprobe` plus frame dimensions and duration.

### Review Order

1. Review still geometry against the water mask and source path.
2. Review short black-screen additive loops.
3. Review one conservative overlay on neutral footage.
4. Review Resolume opacity/blend only after geometry and common fate pass.
5. Bring to Austin as water-only grammar before figures, tankers, or
   supernatural subjects.

### Austin Boundary

Water is the safest internal lead because Austin explained and showed water
movement, but it is still review-needed. Public use, cultural framing, ocean
scaling, mist/cloud grammar, and any claim about meaning require Austin's
per-output OK.

## 3. Topology / Cymatics

Scope: seed/flower topology, cymatic standing waves, sun/ripple/snowflake
fields, radial water geometry, and extracted circle/crescent/trigon-like cells.

### Pass Criteria

- The pass names its topology source: seed/flower circle scaffold, standing
  wave field, ripple impact, snowflake symmetry, sun/ray construction, or other
  explicit mathematical substrate.
- Every visible topology-derived primitive has a `region_type` label using the
  taxonomy names where relevant: `outer_3circle_scallop`, `vesica_lens`,
  `outer_2circle_crescent`, `recursive_crescent_edge`, or another declared
  class.
- Sun/ray trigons derive from outward-attached cells such as
  `outer_3circle_scallop`; they do not use `inner_3circle_intersection` as a
  final visible trigon.
- Curvature direction is correct for the chosen region. Outward rays point away
  from the center; inward debug intersections remain debug-only.
- Construction circles are hidden in the final read or shown only as
  low-opacity debug.
- Radial or cymatic symmetry serves a water/topology function. It is not
  presented as generic sacred geometry or ceremonial meaning.
- Detached sun rays, floating crescents, or unexplained petals are rejected
  unless explicitly labeled as a failed/debug comparison.

### Fail / Anti-Patterns

- Generic sacred geometry, mandala, rosette, flower, or sixfold pattern used
  because it looks impressive.
- Visible trigons from the wrong topology region.
- Construction circles becoming the final visual body.
- Detached rays that do not touch, tuck under, or share the orb/center
  perimeter in sun/ray studies.
- Straight center-to-center triangles filled as final primitives.
- No classifier/debug overlay proving which region produced each visible mark.
- Cymatic dots or petals scattered with no node/antinode, wavefront, or field
  explanation.

### Required Debug / Contact Sheet Evidence

- Contact sheet with construction layer, classified-cell overlay, selected
  visible cells, rejected cells, and final render.
- Sidecar JSON/CSV listing parent circle IDs or wave-field IDs, `region_type`,
  centroid, curvature direction, confidence, and render status.
- Counts by region type and rejected category.
- Debug label or color key for every visible topology class.
- If animated, midpoint contact sheet plus `ffprobe` for all MP4s.

### Review Order

1. Review classifier/debug stills first.
2. Review static final stills only after region labels are correct.
3. Review minimal black-screen loops.
4. Consider audio-reactive cymatics only after the still/loop geometry passes.
5. Present to Austin as an internal topology question, not a cultural claim.

### Austin Boundary

This lane is internal mathematical extraction and morphology reference only.
Austin-derived sun/ray behavior, exact source-color reuse, James Harry 3D
translation, relief shading, and any public topology/cymatic use require
Austin review and per-output approval. Replace "sacred geometry" with "radial
topology" in review-facing language unless there is a specific reason not to.

## 4. Prompt / Dream Scenes

Scope: deterministic prompt-to-primitive templates for visitor dreams or simple
scene prompts. This excludes broad SD/LoRA/style-transfer generation as the
core visual system.

### Pass Criteria

- Prompt parsing produces a bounded scene graph or recipe, not an open-ended
  image prompt.
- Scene elements map to approved internal roles: water state, impact, current,
  mist, rain, topology, neutral landscape, or low-cultural-load industrial
  object for private Pravin review.
- Visible primitives still follow water, path, topology, or scene-graph
  structure. Prompt content does not excuse decorative scatter.
- Safety filters block named/specific people, chiefs, supernatural beings,
  Thunderbird, serpent, crest-like figures, exact Austin source use, and
  unapproved animal identity claims.
- The scene reads through motion and role, not through crude icons. A tanker,
  whale, fish, or bird must not be a simple symbol pasted into the frame.
- Every recipe carries cultural status and public-export status.
- The output can be reviewed without claiming visitor dreams have been
  culturally interpreted.

### Fail / Anti-Patterns

- Raw generative prompt output with no deterministic recipe or safety log.
- Generic whale/fish/tanker symbols presented as primitive scenes.
- Prompt words generating supernatural, crest-like, named-person, or
  Austin-source-adjacent imagery.
- Decorative primitive scatter used to make a weak scene look more active.
- No parse log, no rejected-term list, and no scene-graph overlay.
- Any public-facing wording that says the system translates dreams into Coast
  Salish meaning.

### Required Debug / Contact Sheet Evidence

- Original prompt, normalized prompt, parse log, filter log, and rejected terms.
- Recipe JSON with scene nodes, primitive roles, path/topology bindings,
  cultural status, and public-export status.
- Contact sheet showing final frame, scene graph overlay, path/topology overlay,
  and template reference.
- MP4 `ffprobe` and image dimensions/counts where applicable.
- Review note stating whether the pass is Austin review, Pravin-only technical
  review, or parked.

### Review Order

1. Review parser, filters, and recipe JSON before visuals.
2. Review still scene-graph layouts.
3. Review short black-screen or neutral-background loops.
4. Show industrial/tanker or world-content stress tests to Pravin only until
   framing is settled.
5. Show Austin only bounded water-state or template questions after the safety
   and cultural boundary framing is explicit.

### Austin Boundary

Visitor-prompt and dream-scene systems require Austin review before public use.
Tankers/industry may be a possible direction based on the meeting discussion,
but every output remains internal until Austin approves the specific framing.
No named people, chiefs, supernatural figures, Thunderbird, serpent, or
crest-like subjects are allowed by default.

## 5. Functional Figures

Scope: fish, salmon, birds, flocking, swimming, schooling, current-following,
emergence from water geometry, or figure-like forms. Static Austin-like salmon
replication is parked.

### Pass Criteria

- The figure has a functional reason to exist in the show: swim any direction,
  school/flock, follow currents, emerge from water geometry, return to a source
  composition, or test an explicitly approved rig capability.
- Water/path/topology drives the figure. The figure is not pasted on top of the
  water grammar.
- Figure and water layers remain separable for review and Resolume kill-switch
  control.
- Body structure is labeled: spine, heading, joint/anchor, eye if present,
  fin/tail/wing, negative-space channel, wake, flock vector, or current
  coupling.
- Smooth body mass, negative-space channel, and directionality are more
  important than dense primitive decoration.
- Track 1 procedural Austin-informed function and Track 2 exact Austin-source
  animation remain permanently separate.
- If no functional capability is being tested, the output is parked rather than
  improved cosmetically.

### Fail / Anti-Patterns

- Static fish with no functional value.
- Generic fish glyphs, generic birds, or prettier static salmon copies.
- Exact Austin-source composition copied, traced, or animated without explicit
  source-use clearance.
- Circles/ovals placed near bodies without role labels, creating accidental
  eyes, joints, roe, or body-center claims.
- Fins, wings, rays, or feather cuts invented as cultural grammar without
  Austin review.
- Fish/bird motion independent of current, path, flock, or body spine.
- Any Wolf <-> Thunderbird-Background morph, Thunderbird scene, serpent scene,
  or supernatural figure.

### Required Debug / Contact Sheet Evidence

- Contact sheet with neutral final, alpha/final over water if applicable,
  spine/heading overlay, current-coupling overlay, and flock/path overlay.
- Sidecar data with figure ID, path ID, heading, spine control points, phase,
  current-sample values, wake relation, and layer separation status.
- Functional-state frames: straight swim, turn, current-follow, school/flock,
  emerge, and return where those capabilities are claimed.
- Source-boundary note saying whether the pass is procedural internal,
  Austin-review-needed, exact-source blocked, or approved by a separate record.
- MP4 `ffprobe`, frame dimensions, frame count, and image-count evidence.

### Review Order

1. Decide whether a figure is needed at all for the show moment.
2. Review the functional capability in neutral style before style refinement.
3. Review water coupling and layer separation.
4. Review Austin-informed morphology only after the function passes.
5. Review exact-source animation only after Austin gives explicit source-use
   clearance and per-output approval.

### Austin Boundary

All fish, salmon, bird, eye, joint, fin, wing, body-center, roe, sun-face, and
figure grammar is Austin-review-needed. Static salmon studies are parked unless
they become functional rig evidence. Exact Austin-source animation, source-like
composition, and public figure use are blocked until Austin gives explicit
approval for that output.
