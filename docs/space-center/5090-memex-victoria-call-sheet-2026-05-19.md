## No-Go Conditions

Do not proceed without a new decision if any of these are true:

- RTX 5090 is no longer available / cannot be held.
- Build cannot realistically be ready by Friday 2026-05-22.
- PSU is under 1200W.
- GPU power requires an adapter instead of a native PSU-to-GPU 12V-2x6 cable.
- Case fit or cable bend is uncertain and the build team cannot verify a safer case.
- RAM substitution is 4x16GB instead of 2x32GB.
- They want to switch to Windows 11 Home for convenience.

## Acceptable Substitutions

- **CPU:** 9950X3D is fine; plain 9950X is also fine if it helps cost / stock / schedule.
- **RAM:** 64GB DDR5-6000 EXPO, 2x32GB. CL30 is nice; CL36 is acceptable.
- **Case:** any larger airflow case that clearly fits RTX 5090 + 360mm AIO + 1200W PSU + safe cable bend.
- **Storage:** 2TB Gen4 NVMe is preferred. Do not chase Gen5 upsell.
- **PSU:** 1200W+ Gold / Platinum, native 12V-2x6, no adapter. 1300W is fine if reliable and available.
