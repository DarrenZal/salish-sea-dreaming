## Purpose

Define the emerging installation architecture as an anchor graph before any
follow-up beauty renders or review packets. The graph keeps the team from
treating every visual experiment as a candidate lead: each probe must name its
node type, edge type, source/provenance status, review boundary, and pass/park
reason.

The current working thesis is that the installation is not one renderer. It is
a set of anchored transitions among wave fields, water-cycle phrase states,
whole Austin artwork nodes, and spatial artwork treatments.

## Core Framing

The validated distinction is that Austin works remain whole authored works.
Shared anchors are alignment metadata only: center points, sun-disc radius,
axes, source bounding boxes, masks, timing, and reveal order. The renderer is
not decomposing Austin's art into reusable atoms or motifs.

This is the material difference from the earlier atom-morph approach. In the
current portal architecture, `Nature_Cosmic_Sun.svg` and
`Animal_Bird_Raven_Sun.svg` stay intact as authored source compositions. The
shared sun-disc anchor does not become a renderable motif, a style token, or a
generic sun primitive.

## Canonical Transition Pattern

The canonical transition pattern is:

```text
Artwork -> anchor collapse -> cymatic field -> target gather -> whole artwork reveal
```

The cymatic field is the universal connective tissue between artwork nodes. An
artwork does not have to morph directly into every other artwork. Instead, it
collapses through its own measured anchor metadata into an abstract cymatic
field, and the target artwork gathers from that same kind of field through its
own measured anchor metadata.

This avoids requiring direct N x N artwork morphs. Each artwork node needs only
two contracts:

1. Collapse to cymatic field.
2. Reveal from cymatic field.

Those contracts are technical display contracts, not cultural claims. Whole
Austin artworks remain whole authored sources. Anchors remain metadata only:
center points, radii, axis counts, aperture order, masks, scale, timing, and
bounds. Generated cymatic fields are abstract/math/water atmosphere, not Coast
Salish artwork.

Hard boundary:

- The system must not generate or claim to generate Coast Salish designs.
- Generated visuals may be described only as abstract cymatic fields, water
  geometry, primitive-like studies, or atmospheric support.
- Cultural content must come from Austin-authored or Austin-approved works.

## Edge Notes

### 1. Wave -> Artwork Portal

Status: VALIDATED INTERNAL PROBE.

Evidence: `wave_to_artwork_portal_v002`.

What is validated:

- The source configuration matches the target artwork symmetry: an 8-source
  octagonal gather fits the eightfold radial rhythm of `Nature_Cosmic_Sun.svg`.
- The central orb aperture opens first, ray apertures follow, and the whole
  source appears through a circular/radial aperture.
- The output removes the rectangular-card read at peak.
- Austin's artwork remains whole-source: no training, diffusion, style
  imitation, motif fragmentation, or atom reuse.

Caveats:

- The portal moment could hold longer.
- The peak could retain more subtle wave presence behind, around, or at the
  rim of the artwork.
- The alignment is radial/positional rather than full shape equivalence.
  Austin's concave trigon geometry is carried by the authored artwork, not
  generated by the wave field.

Decision:

- Keep Wave -> artwork portal as a validated internal edge.
- Do not commission another v003 beauty render yet. The next decision is the
  review packet architecture, not visual polish.

### 2. Artwork -> Artwork Anchored Portal

Status: VALIDATED / PROVISIONAL INTERNAL PROBE.

Evidence: `anchored_artwork_portal_chain_v001`.

What is validated:

- The chain can carry continuity from wave geometry into
  `Nature_Cosmic_Sun.svg`, then into `Animal_Bird_Raven_Sun.svg`, using a
  stable sun-disc center/radius anchor.
- The transition is not a slideshow crossfade: it uses source-aligned center,
  radius, mask, axis, and scaling metadata.
- Both Austin works remain whole authored sources. The renderer is not
  decomposing either work into reusable atoms or motifs.

Why provisional:

- The output is internal only and pending Austin review.
- The pass is technical/aesthetic, not cultural. It does not assert that the
  shared sun-disc anchor has cultural meaning or that the transition is
  externally cleared.

Decision:

- Keep as a provisional internal edge.
- Use this edge in the Austin architectural review packet only as a bounded
  question about whole-source portal continuity and anchor choice.

### 3. Artwork -> Spatial / 3D Treatment

Status: VALIDATED FOR FIGURE-ON-FIELD PIECES; PARK FOR DENSE
RADIAL/TESSELLATED PIECES.

Evidence: Raven Sun 3D extrusion/parallax passes; Cosmic Sun 3D extrusion has
integrity but modest payoff.

What is validated:

- The renderer preserves Austin's geometry and palette.
- It uses authored SVG grouping only, not atom-level decomposition.
- `Animal_Bird_Raven_Sun.svg` produces spatial moments a flat source cannot:
  the figure separates from the field, the raven shifts against the sun, and
  the mid landscape/cloud layer moves at a different rate.

What is parked:

- `Nature_Cosmic_Sun.svg` as a parallax lead. The dense radial/tessellated
  composition preserves integrity but does not produce a compelling depth
  payoff.

Decision:

- 3D spatial treatment is strongest when the authored source already separates
  figure, field, and depth.
- Dense radial artworks should be treated as portal targets, not parallax
  targets, unless Austin later requests a spatial study.

## General Principles

1. Source configuration must match target artwork symmetry.
2. Aperture reveal order should follow target artwork structure.
3. Whole authored artwork remains whole; anchors are alignment metadata, not
   renderable motifs.
4. 3D spatial treatment works best when authored source layers already
   separate figure, field, and depth.
5. Dense radial artworks are better as portal targets than parallax targets.
6. Future Austin communication should frame him as curator of narrative and
   anchor choices, not reviewer of generated Coast Salish-style outputs.
