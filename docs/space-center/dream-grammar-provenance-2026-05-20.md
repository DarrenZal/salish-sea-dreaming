## Shape Vocabulary

| Shape label | Internal role | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `circle-like` | Origin, impact, pivot, eddy knot, orb, soft anchor; joint/eye only under review. | Avoid unlabeled dots, accidental voids, paired-eye reads, or roe-like clusters unless flagged. |
| `crescent-like` | Cupping, phase, wake, current mark, rotation, mist arc; body/wing arcs under review. | Must relate to an origin, path, bend, body, or membrane. No decorative moon-stamp repeats. |
| `trigon-like` | Release, attenuation, point, flow tip; ray/fin/head/tail under review. | Must not become a generic triangle, UI arrow, play button, spike, or detached scatter mark. |
| `S-crescent` | Flow bend, current band, wake arc, mist drift; body channel under review. | Must follow a declared path/body/current relation, not decorative fill. |
| `curved trigon` | Release cell, attenuation cell, ray cell, fin/tail cell under review. | Rear edge must attach to a crescent, line, orb, body, or membrane without accidental circle-sized gaps. |
| `oval` | Stretched origin, eddy anchor, membrane boundary, soft orb; body core/eye under review. | Elongation must be purposeful: shear, axis, membrane pressure, eddy stretch, or review-labeled figure use. |
| `smooth mass` | Water sheet, cloud field, beach/forest mass, terrain mask; body silhouette under review. | Must not silently become a copied Austin silhouette, portrait, animal body, mask, crest, or supernatural being. |
| `line/path` | Flow spine, flock/school path, shoreline, river axis, construction guide. | Construction paths should not appear as final imagery unless intentionally reviewed. |

## Core Rules

Default water phrase:

```text
circle-like origin -> crescent-like phase -> crescent-like phase -> curved trigon release
```

Operational rules:

- Use phrase order before density.
- Keep primitive placement path-led, origin-led, boundary-led, or topology-led.
- Keep broad water fields sparse; do not wallpaper the scene.
- Make crescents cup or phase around a declared relation.
- Make trigons curved, attached, and directional.
- Preserve negative-space and gap discipline.
- Keep visible marks inside declared water, cloud, terrain, body, membrane, or review masks unless labeled as spray, foam, ray spill, mist, or review exception.
- Treat fish, birds, whales, people, bodies, eyes, faces, fins, wings, joints, rays, and silhouettes as Austin-review-needed by default.
- Block exact Austin source replication unless Austin explicitly approves that exact use.

## Subject Mappings

| Subject | Mapping |
|---|---|
| Water | Safest internal lead, still review-needed. Use circle/crescent/crescent/curved-trigon phrase, S-crescents, ovals, smooth water masks, and line/path flow spines. |
| Sun | Internal review-needed. Use central circle-like orb with attached crescent/ray/release marks only as a question. No sun face details, raven-sun relation, exact palette, or detached ray scatter. |
| Rain | Internal review-needed. Sparse impact phrases; each drop can have a small origin and short attenuation. No dense particle wallpaper. |
| Snow | Internal review-needed. Sparse attached radial or harmonic cells only as a weather/topology question. No sacred-geometry framing or full lattice aesthetic. |
| Salmon/fish | Functional motion only. Prefer current-following, schooling, wake, tail-lag, and water-driven phrases over fish glyphs. Static Austin-like salmon replication is parked. |
| Birds | Internal review-needed. Favor flock path and common-fate motion over species-specific silhouette, raven identity, eye, beak, or wing detail. |
| Orca/whale | Internal review-needed. Favor breach path, water impact, spray, wake, and silhouette-safe abstraction before any body detail. |
| Human figures | Public-blocked by default. Schema-only or non-identifiable placeholders unless a review path exists. Named people, chiefs, portraits, identifiable children, and faces are blocked. |
| Beach | Environmental container. Use shoreline boundary, wave edge, smooth land/water masks. Human blockers govern prompts with people or children. |
| Forest | Environmental mass/canopy structure. Avoid beings, masks, faces, crests, or supernatural inference. |
| River | Water subtype. Use line/path river axis, smooth water band, S-crescents, sparse phrases at bends, rocks, eddies, and constrictions. |
| Clouds | Review question. Mist/fog may use low-contrast partial phrases along drift paths, but there is no approved dedicated cloud grammar. |

## Forbidden Or Paused Areas
