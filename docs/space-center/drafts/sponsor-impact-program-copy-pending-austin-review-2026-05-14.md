**Salish Sea Dreaming: Inside the Pearl**

Salish Sea Dreaming is a three-screen immersive artwork for Indigenomics IMPACT at the HR MacMillan Space Centre. This phase is co-led with Austin Harry of INDIGITAL, whose digital Coast Salish design practice brings ancestral form, contemporary tools, and public-space media into conversation.

The May installation is organized around Austin's pearl vision: Thunderbird shares a pearl, and inside it three design forms move, breathe, and transform. The triptych treats the three projectors as windows into that single pearl interior rather than as three separate screens.

Western scientific signals, including tide and Fraser River flow, sit beside underwater footage and Austin-approved formline morphing. The point is not to translate Coast Salish knowledge into data, but to let different ways of sensing place remain distinct while sharing one field of attention.

AI tools are used only inside a consented workflow: source material is curated, outputs are reviewed, and public use requires artist approval. For sponsors, this version is a working prototype for a larger path from triptych installation toward future interactive and fulldome experiences.
