## [PENDING AUSTIN REVIEW] Assembled Draft

Phase 2 of Salish Sea Dreaming is a compressed May sprint that adapts the Phase 1 underwater and interactive system into a three-projector triptych for Indigenomics IMPACT at the HR MacMillan Space Centre.

The creative container is now Austin Harry's pearl vision: Thunderbird shares a pearl, and the viewer is invited into the pearl where three design forms swirl, morph, and teach. Operationally, this means the screens should read as three windows into one continuous pearl interior, with the center panel carrying the most explicit lineage layer.

The left and right panels can hold live and recorded bioregional material: tide level, tide rate, Fraser River flow, Moonfish underwater footage, and water-texture motion. The center panel should prioritize Austin-approved forms, deterministic primitive morphing, and reviewed public language over unconstrained generative imagery.

The v2 LoRA path is a style-transfer research layer, not a license to invent new crests or publish unscreened images. The safest production path remains Track 2: decompose Austin's actual approved vector work into primitives, review labels and morph pairs with him, then animate only approved mappings.

StreamDiffusion / SDTD can still become a live atmospheric layer if the v2 model passes still evaluation and Austin approves representative outputs. Any sponsor, venue, social, or press language should use the consent map as its source of truth, especially around "teaching," "pearl," "Thunderbird passing," and "Coast Salish design forms."
