## [PENDING AUSTIN REVIEW] Assembled Draft

**Salish Sea Dreaming: Inside the Pearl**

For this Space Centre version, Salish Sea Dreaming becomes three windows into one shared interior world. The work follows a vision offered by co-leading artist Austin Harry: a Thunderbird sharing a pearl that carries three Coast Salish design forms in motion.

Inside that pearl, line, water, and ecological signal breathe together across the triptych. The left and right screens carry live and recorded traces of the Salish Sea: tide, river flow, underwater footage, and the movement of water. The center screen holds the lineage layer, where Austin-approved forms can be decomposed, transformed, and recomposed without inventing new crests.

AI is used here as a constrained production tool under artist direction, not as a replacement for cultural authorship. Every public output using Austin's work is subject to his review and approval.
