## Motif Atlas

### S-Crescents

Description: S-crescents are bent, comma-like, or paired crescent forms that bend around a body, current, wing, or coil rather than sitting as isolated moon shapes. They often appear as nested interior marks, scale-like body details, tentacle bands, wing cuts, or curved phase marks.

Observed sources:

- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Salmon_Spawn_Eggs.jpg` - nested crescent marks inside the two salmon bodies, with pale S-like negative channels running along each body.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Water_Octopus_Transparent.jpg` - tentacles form large S-curves with smaller crescent marks riding along the arms.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Supernatural_Snake_Serpent.jpg` - body coils and rib marks show repeated S-flow, but this source is public-blocked for direct use.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Bird_Raven_Transparent.jpg` - wing and body details use crescent cuts that follow the wing sweep.
- `austin-v2-ingest/decomposed/Animal_Salmon_Spawn_Eggs/atom_metadata.csv` - internal labels include 10 `crescent` atoms.
- `austin-v2-ingest/decomposed/Animal_Wolf_Spindle_Whorl/atom_metadata.csv` - internal labels include 16 `crescent` atoms, but wolf source has high cultural caution.

Could inspire:

- Water: current bands that follow an S-curve river or eddy path.
- Fish: body phase marks along a spine, but only after Austin reviews fish morphology.
- Bird: wing feather arcs and cloud/sky dissolves.
- Network: curved edge segments in a field graph, avoiding decorative scatter.

Boundary: use as an internal shape-quality reference only. Do not copy exact salmon, wolf, octopus, serpent, or raven crescents into new public work. Serpent and supernatural sources remain blocked without explicit Austin guidance.

### Ovoids

Description: Ovoids appear as eyes, body centers, joints, membrane anchors, sun-face features, and large internal fill forms. They are rarely neutral decoration; their role depends on placement and adjacency.

Observed sources:

- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Salmon_Spawn_Eggs.jpg` - eye ovoids, central focal orb, body ovoids, and egg/roe circles.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Nature_Cosmic_Sun.jpg` - paired eye ovoids and a central mouth ovoid inside a dominant sun orb.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Water_Orca_Transparent.jpg` - eye ovoids, body ovoids, and large white belly negative-space ovoids.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Bear_Background.jpg`, `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Deer_Background.jpg`, and `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Wolf_Background.jpg` - body ovoids and joint-like ovoids on limbs.
- `austin-v2-ingest/decomposed/Supernatural_Human_TheCreator_Background/atom_metadata.csv` - 31 `circle-oval` atoms and 3 `eye-focal-oval` atoms; use only as a cautionary high-load source.

Could inspire:

- Water: eddy centers, soft anchors, and impact origins.
- Fish: eye or body anchor placement after Austin review.
- Bird: one focal throat/eye anchor, not many repeated eyes.
- Sun: central orb and face-feature hierarchy.
- Network: node anchors, but with explicit role labels to avoid accidental eyes or roe.

Boundary: paired ovoids quickly read as eyes or faces. Public or review-facing output must not present ovoid placement as culturally valid without Austin approval.

### Smooth Body Masses

Description: Many sources use large smooth silhouettes as the primary body, then cut or overlay smaller forms inside. The body is not assembled from many tiny pieces; it reads first as a confident mass.

Observed sources:

- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Salmon_Spawn_Eggs.jpg` - each salmon has a broad head/shoulder mass tapering into tail structure.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Water_Orca_Transparent.jpg` - one sweeping whale body arc with large negative belly shape.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Water_Octopus_Transparent.jpg` - mantle and tentacles are smooth, continuous, and rounded.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Bear_Background.jpg` - body block reads as a single seated mass with internal marks.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Bird_Raven_Sun.jpg` - raven silhouette is a strong single black figure over the sun.

Could inspire:

- Fish: start from a smooth tapered body or spine envelope before adding detail.
- Bird: wing/body silhouettes should read before feather marks.
- Water: broad sheet or membrane first, primitives second.
- Network: large field regions can contain nodes and arcs without becoming wallpaper.

Boundary: do not approximate exact animal silhouettes for public work. New internal glyphs should remain visibly procedural sketches unless Austin approves exact-source use.

### Negative-Space Channels

Description: Pale or open channels carve through bodies, wings, tails, and field layers. These channels carry direction, separation, and breath; they should not be casually filled.

Observed sources:

- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Salmon_Spawn_Eggs.jpg` - long pale channels separate belly/back flows and define the two-fish rotational relationship.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Bird_Raven_Transparent.jpg` - dark wing mass is cut by angular black/blue negative wedges.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Water_Orca_Transparent.jpg` - large white belly channel dominates the body read.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Insect_Butterfly_Transparent.jpg` - wing cells and white edge spots create tight negative-space rhythm.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Supernatural_Transformer_Background.jpg` - diagonal field uses high-contrast negative channels, but this source is public-blocked.

Could inspire:

- Water: channels of open water around glyph marks; avoid filling every surface.
- Fish: body separation and wake channels instead of dense anatomy.
- Bird: wing slits and feather rhythm.
- Sun: open radial wedges between rays.
- Network: empty corridors between clusters so the field breathes.

Boundary: negative-space treatment can approach exact-source identity quickly. Use the principle of preserved open channels, not copied channel shapes.

### Radial Suns

Description: Radial suns organize a dominant circle/orb, ray wedges, pointed trigons, and sometimes facial ovoids. The radial grammar has strong center-out hierarchy.

Observed sources:

- `track2-deterministic/source-vectors/Nature_Cosmic_Sun.svg` and `austin-v2-ingest/training/Nature_Cosmic_Sun.jpg` - dominant central orb, large wedges, red-orange points, internal face ovoids/crescents.
- `track2-deterministic/source-vectors/Animal_Bird_Raven_Sun.svg` and `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Bird_Raven_Sun.jpg` - sun disk behind raven; rays frame the figure.
- `austin-v2-ingest/decomposed/Nature_Cosmic_Sun/atom_metadata.csv` - labels include 10 `sun-ray`, 10 `trigon`, 8 `crescent`, 4 `circle-oval`, and 2 `eye-focal-oval`.
- `austin-v2-ingest/decomposed/Animal_Bird_Raven_Sun/atom_metadata.csv` - labels include 5 `sun-ray` and 10 `trigon` atoms.

Could inspire:

- Sun: circle-origin plus radial trigon/ray studies.
- Water: ripple fields radiating from an impact point.
- Bird: silhouette crossing a sun field, only as an internal composition question.
- Network: radial hub-and-spoke field layouts, not random stars.

Boundary: Austin-derived sun/ray behavior is review-needed. Exact sun face, raven-sun relationship, and public sun grammar require per-output Austin approval.

### Roe / Circle Fields

Description: Circle fields appear as roe, stars, snow-like particles, or background orbs. The strongest examples use variable size, edge cropping, and non-grid placement around a main composition.

Observed sources:

- `track2-deterministic/source-vectors/Animal_Salmon_Spawn_Eggs.svg` - 184 circle elements in the source SVG.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Salmon_Spawn_Eggs.jpg` - roe/circle field wraps the fish composition with varied scale and large cropped edge circles.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Supernatural_Human_TheCreator_Background.jpg` - star/circle field above the figure; public-blocked source, useful only as caution.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Deer_Background.jpg` - small sun/star-like field points around a centered animal.
- `austin-v2-ingest/decomposed/Animal_Salmon_Spawn_Eggs/atom_metadata.csv` - labels include 184 `egg-roe` atoms.

Could inspire:

- Water: impact bubbles, rain hits, foam seeds, or eddy nodes if clearly water-labeled.
- Fish: roe/spawning only after Austin review.
- Sun/network: variable-size nodes and field density around a center.

Boundary: roe is visually specific in the salmon source. Do not generalize egg/roe circles as a particle system or public motif without Austin approval. Avoid tiny decorative scatter and grid placement.

### Animal Joints

Description: Circles and ovoids often sit at eyes, shoulders, hips, limbs, or interior body anchors. They articulate structure rather than decorate the body.

Observed sources:

- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Bear_Background.jpg` - limb ovoids and paw/joint anchors inside a heavy body.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Deer_Background.jpg` - leg and body ovoids mark articulation points.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Wolf_Background.jpg` - leg/hip circles and interior body anchors.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Insect_Bee.jpg` - small segmented legs and antennae articulate from a central body.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Water_Orca_Transparent.jpg` - fins and tail connect through broad body anchors.

Could inspire:

- Fish: one or two explicit anchors for eye/joint/body after review.
- Bird: wing root or throat anchor, not repeated eye-like dots.
- Network: pivot nodes on paths.
- Water: eddy pivots and current knots.

Boundary: figure joints are Austin-review-needed. Circles near bodies can read as eyes, joints, or spiritual/animal identity; keep internal and label roles precisely.

### Fins / Wings / Rays

Description: Directional appendages use tapered points, crescent arcs, trigon cuts, and wedge arrays. They point, release, flap, fan, or radiate from a body/orb.

Observed sources:

- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Salmon_Spawn_Eggs.jpg` - tail/fin structures taper into pointed crescent/trigon-like endings.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Bird_Raven_Sun.jpg` - broad wings arc upward with repeated triangular feather cuts; sun rays sit behind.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Bird_Heron_Background.jpg` - wing mass carries nested crescent feather marks.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Insect_Bee.jpg` and `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Insect_Butterfly_Transparent.jpg` - wing membranes, ribbing, and edge ovoids.
- `austin-v2-ingest/decomposed/Animal_Salmon_Spawn_Eggs/atom_metadata.csv` - labels include 2 `fin-tail` atoms and 1 `trigon`.
- `austin-v2-ingest/decomposed/Animal_Bird_Raven_Sun/atom_metadata.csv` - labels include 10 `trigon` and 5 `sun-ray` atoms.

Could inspire:

- Water: trigon release marks at ripple endpoints.
- Fish: tail/fins only after morphology review.
- Bird: wing arcs and flock glyphs as generic internal studies, not raven copies.
- Sun: ray emission and radial pulse behavior.
- Network: directional arrows should be curved trigons, not UI arrows.

Boundary: fins, wings, rays, and feather cuts are figure/ray semantics; all public use and any Austin-derived identity requires per-output review.

### Rotational Compositions

Description: Rotational composition organizes bodies or fields around a center without simple mirror symmetry. It can produce chase, orbit, spindle, sun, or current-knot reads.

Observed sources:

- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Salmon_Spawn_Eggs.jpg` - two salmon form a yin-yang-like rotation around a central focal orb.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Wolf_Spindle_Whorl.jpg` - two-fold rotational wolf/spindle composition; high cultural caution.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Nature_Cosmic_Sun.jpg` - radial sun with center-out organization.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Water_Octopus_Transparent.jpg` - tentacles radiate and curl from a central mantle.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Bird_Raven_Sun.jpg` - radial sun behind a centered bird silhouette.

Could inspire:

- Water: eddies, current knots, whirlpool-like phrase placement.
- Fish: school orbit or two-body chase only after Austin review.
- Bird: flock bank around a center, but avoid raven identity.
- Sun: radial pulse studies.
- Network: orbiting clusters around a node.

Boundary: rotational animal compositions can imply exact source narratives or beings. Keep rotational studies abstract unless Austin reviews the specific source relation.

### Network / Field-Like Compositions

Description: Field compositions use repeated points, stars, cells, ribs, or boundary clusters to create a distributed environment around the main form. The key difference from bad scatter is that density follows composition: edge, center, path, body, or sky.

Observed sources:

- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Salmon_Spawn_Eggs.jpg` - variable roe field wraps and frames the rotational salmon pair.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Supernatural_Human_TheCreator_Background.jpg` - black sky field with stars and small orbs; very high caution.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Deer_Background.jpg` - small sun/snow/star symbols distributed across the mountain field.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Insect_Butterfly_Transparent.jpg` - wing cells and edge spots create a controlled bilateral field.
- `austin-v2-ingest/training/Supernatural_Transformer_Background.jpg` - diagonal high-contrast field with dots and crescent/trigon chains; public-blocked.

Could inspire:

- Water: foam fields, rain impacts, current knots, or stream-node maps.
- Fish: spawning context only after Austin review.
- Bird: flock density fields, not literal raven replication.
- Sun: corona, star fields, or ray-node fields.
- Network: primitive graph layouts with path-led density and open negative channels.

Boundary: field-like does not mean random. No tiny decorative tiling, no grid scatter, no generic "Austin style" particle fill, and no public use of roe/star fields without Austin per-output approval.

## Design Translation By Study Lane

### Water

Use source-inspired structure, not source copying:

- Lead with path, origin, and boundary: circle impact or eddy anchor -> cupping crescents -> trigon release.
- Let S-crescents follow current paths and topology bends.
- Keep negative-space channels open; large water sheets should breathe.
- Use circle fields only when role-labeled as rain, bubbles, foam, or current nodes.
- Avoid turning water into tiled decorative primitives.

Approval boundary: water remains the safest internal lead, but generated water grammar is still not public-approved.

### Fish

Useful inspiration:

- Smooth tapered body masses from `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Salmon_Spawn_Eggs.jpg`.
- Negative-space channels through the body.
- Sparse ovoids for eye/body anchors.
- Tail/fin taper as directional release.
- Rotational two-body composition as a question, not a default.

Approval boundary: salmon morphology requires a separate Austin-informed still pass. Trajectory/wake marks are not salmon bodies. Exact salmon source animation requires Austin approval.

### Bird

Useful inspiration:

- Wing arc first, feather detail second.
- One directional head/beak vector rather than neutral symmetry.
- Negative cuts along wings as rhythm.
- Bird-in-front-of-sun composition as an internal question from `austin-v2-ingest/training/Animal_Bird_Raven_Sun.jpg`.

Approval boundary: do not copy raven silhouette for generic flock work. Thunderbird and supernatural bird sources are blocked until Austin gives direct guidance.

### Sun / Sky

Useful inspiration:

- Central orb with strong radial hierarchy.
- Ray wedges and trigon points around the circle.
- Low-contrast field rays behind the main disk.
- Face/eye ovoids only as exact-source or review-needed elements.

Approval boundary: exact `austin-v2-ingest/training/Nature_Cosmic_Sun.jpg` face animation and Austin-derived sun/ray behavior need review before public use.

### Network / Field

Useful inspiration:

- Variable-size nodes with edge cropping from the salmon roe field.
- Star / orb fields as density studies, not semantic claims.
- Butterfly wing-cell organization for bounded bilateral fields.
- Path-led chains of crescent/trigon marks from transformer-like field references, but avoid the supernatural source itself.

Approval boundary: network studies should stay abstract and internal. Do not present roe, stars, or nodes as traditional meanings.
