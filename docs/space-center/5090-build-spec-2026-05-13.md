# Salish Sea Dreaming — RTX 5090 Build Spec (Phase 2+)

**Date:** 2026-05-13
**Audience:** Natalia (curator-financing artist-project sponsorship pipeline) and Pravin
**Purpose:** Specs + cost for the proposed SSD Phase 2 production workstation, for sponsor outreach

## Why this build

Phase 2 (Indigenomics IMPACT @ HR MacMillan Space Centre, late May 2026) is the proof-of-concept for evolving Salish Sea Dreaming into a full-dome interactive experience (MOVE37XR Oct 2026 → DEVCON Nov 2026 → Life at Center 2027). The current single RTX 3090 (24 GB VRAM) handles the Phase 1 Mahon Hall stack but is at its limits for the Phase 2 quality targets:

- Three matched 1080p projectors (or 4K stretch) instead of one
- Live AI style transfer in TouchDesigner StreamDiffusion in Austin Harry's visual register (the "make-or-break" production-quality requirement for the Space Centre's sophisticated, sponsor-facing audience)
- Co-leading-artist pipeline with TELUS H200 cloud rendering for pre-rendered hero clips
- Headroom for dome-resolution rendering when the show migrates

The RTX 5090's 32 GB VRAM and roughly 2× the compute over the 3090 is the next-step workstation that lets the same single-machine architecture scale through the Phase 2.x venue arc without rebuilding. Vancouver retail availability has stabilized; this is the right moment to acquire.

### Honest range and what could shift it

- **Lower end (~$10,500 turnkey):** drop to 64 GB RAM (–$300), skip secondary 4 TB SSD (–$560), cheaper case (–$110). Still functional for production but tighter on RAM headroom for parallel TD/Autolume/Resolume/SDTD.
- **Higher end (~$13,000 turnkey):** premium AIB GPU variant (Astral / Suprim Liquid X +$300), 128 GB RAM (+$200), 4 TB Gen5 primary (+$300). Maximum future-proofing.

## What this enables for SSD Phase 2

| Capability | RTX 3090 (current) | RTX 5090 (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| Live SD StreamDiffusion at higher steps | 4 steps, sd-turbo only | 8–15 steps, multiple sampler choices |
| Concurrent TD + Autolume + Resolume + SDTD | At ~95% VRAM | ~50% VRAM (room for ControlNet, IP-Adapter, second LoRA) |
| Live 1080p × 3 output | Tight (≈19.5 GB / 24 GB per industry analysis) | Comfortable headroom; supports stretch 4K × 3 |
| Live Austin-register style transfer (Phase 2 "make or break") | Risk of compromise | Production-grade headroom |
| Future full-dome render at 4K+ | Not feasible | Native target |
| LoRA training (currently TELUS-only) | Local only as fallback | Local fast prototyping; TELUS stays for production runs |

## Risk if we don't acquire by EOD this week
