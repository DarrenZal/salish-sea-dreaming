## Direction

Build one abstract water-cycle scene language:

```text
rain drop -> radial water wave -> six-ray snowflake -> cloud/mist -> sun/radiant field -> rain drop
```

The cycle is not meteorology, ceremony, or sacred geometry. It is an internal
motion system where water states share the same primitive morphology:

- circle: origin, droplet, node, orb, antinode body;
- crescent: ripple phase, cupping arc, lens, mist arc, shear/wake mark;
- trigon: release, ray tip, attenuation, spray, three-region cell.

## State Morphology

| State | Field source | Circle role | Crescent role | Trigon role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rain drop | Touch/rain impulse or short-lived emitter. | Droplet and impact origin. | First partial ripple arc around impact. | Tiny splash/release or late attenuation mark. |
| Radial water wave | Multi-emitter standing/ripple field. | Center origin or interference node. | Partial rings and lens-like wavefront cells. | Outward release where ripple weakens or three fronts meet. |
| Six-ray snowflake | Circular membrane / Bessel `m=6` harmonic. | Center node or closed frozen-water cell. | Paired lenses along arms; phase arcs between lobes. | Six attached ray tips or three-way angular gaps. |
| Cloud / mist | Weak multi-emitter network with low alpha. | Occasional soft node only; often omitted. | Main visible form: faint drifting partial arcs. | Rare terminal cue at edge of flow network. |
| Sun / radiant field | Central emitter plus `m=0`/`m=6` radial blend. | Central orb/ripple origin. | Ring-phase arcs around the orb. | Attached outward ray/release cells, never detached arrows. |

## Visual Rhythms

Use wave language as rhythm control, not as an overclaim:

- Standing waves: stable nodal outlines plus filled antinodal cells. Animate by
  swapping outline/fill/inverse-fill, not by flashing the whole field.
- Beats: close phase speeds create slow amplitude envelopes. Use the envelope
  to reveal/fade cell families, not to increase density across the whole frame.
- Group velocity: a wave packet or activation window can drift through the
  field while local cells phase in place. Use this to move attention from rain
  impact to ripple, snow, mist, or radiant state.

Allowed language: "standing-wave-inspired rhythm", "beat envelope",
"activation packet", "phase inversion". Do not claim literal sound-to-culture,
literal weather physics, or physical group velocity unless simulated and
labeled.

## Not Sacred-Geometry Wallpaper

This direction differs from sacred-geometry wallpaper because:

- construction circles, seed/flower scaffolds, and harmonic fields are hidden
  generators, not the final subject;
- only sparse classified cells render;
- every visible mark has a role, phrase, phase, boundary, or path reason;
- density caps preserve open water and negative space;
- trigons attach to origins, rings, membranes, or paths;
- the renderer exports debug overlays and sidecar roles before beauty frames;
- "sacred geometry" is not used as approval, cultural framing, or public
  language.

Reject outputs that show a full flower lattice, mandala, rosette, decorative
sixfold field, detached sun rays, generic triangle rays, or all-over particle
rain with no phrase order.

## Austin Review Boundary

phase, and debug studies.

Austin review is needed before:

- any public use of primitive grammar output;
- sun/radiant/ray behavior, especially anything that reads as Austin-derived
  sun grammar;

## Render Recipes

### 1. Agent A - Rain Impact To Radial Wave v001

Source: water footage or neutral black layer with authored impact points.

Recipe:

- Circle droplet appears at impact.
- Two crescent wavefront arcs expand from the origin.
- Trigon appears late on the weakest release edge and fades first.
- Optional optical-flow mask keeps impacts inside water only.

Acceptance:

- Debug still shows source frame, water mask, impact origins, phrase IDs, and
  confidence.
- At least three impacts read as `circle -> crescent -> crescent -> trigon`.
- Crescents cup the origin; trigons point outward.
- No full concentric-ring shader, dense rain particles, or random crescents.

### 2. Agent B - Radial Wave To Six-Ray Snowflake v001

Source: circular membrane/Bessel field, `m=0` blend into `m=6`.

Recipe:

- Start with central circle and radial ripple crescents.
- Phase-lock into six sparse arms.
- Render only selected lens/crescent cells and attached trigon ray tips.

Acceptance:

- Debug overlay names mode, nodal lines, selected cells, and rejected cells.
- Sixfold read is clear but not a mandala.
- Trigon tips remain curved/attached, not triangle spokes.
- Final frame keeps open negative space.

### 3. Agent B - Standing-Wave Phase Inversion v001

Source: analytic multi-emitter standing field.

Recipe:

- Nodal contours stay as outlines.
- Positive antinode cells fill first.
- Fill collapses back to outline.
- Negative/inverse cells fill next.

Acceptance:

- No full-screen flash at zero crossing.
- Circle/crescent/trigon cell classes remain recognizable through the loop.
- Beat envelope changes opacity slowly, not density.
- Contact sheet includes outline, positive fill, outline, inverse fill.

### 4. Agent A - Mist Cloud Partial Phrase v001

Source: weak flow network, authored splines, or low-confidence footage fallback.

Recipe:

- Drop most circle origins.
- Let faint crescents drift along slow network edges.
- Use rare small trigons only at terminal release points.
- Keep the layer low contrast and sparse.

Acceptance:

- Debug overlay shows network edges or streamlines.
- Mist reads as partial phrase, not particle fog.
- No accidental eyes, faces, animals, or dense cloud icons.
- Sidecar labels omitted circles as intentional `origin_hidden`.

### 5. Agent A/B - Sun / Radiant Water Field v001

Source: central emitter plus radial harmonic field; no exact Austin source use.

Recipe:

- Circle orb pulses as water/radiant origin.
- Crescents phase around the orb in partial rings.
- Attached trigons release outward at selected lobes.
- Optional activation packet moves from ripple state into radiant state.

Acceptance:

- Review note says internal radiant/ripple study, not Austin sun approval.
- Rays are attached to center/ring structure and do not float.
- Trigons attenuate by scale/opacity.
- Debug layer can mute radiant/ray cells separately from water/ripple cells.

## Bottom Line

The water-cycle/cymatic direction is strongest when it behaves like one shared
state machine for water forms: impact, ripple, frozen sixfold order, mist, and
radiance. The visible layer must stay sparse, role-labeled, and review-bounded.
