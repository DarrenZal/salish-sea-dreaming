# Budget · AGOG Open Call 2026 · Salish Sea Dreaming

> **Funding request: $150,000 USD.** Right-sized for AGOG's competitive $1M pool (deliberately below the $200K ceiling, per the fiscal sponsor's read that a maxed ask from a shared pool lowers funding odds). Built around the real model: each installation is **custom and contextual to its place**, co-created with **local artists + local ecological data** (what the first two shows did). The largest share funds local-artist co-authorship and local-data co-creation at each of 8 to 10 stops, not the touring of a finished piece. Two completed 2026 shows de-risk the ask. Applicant entity: Department of Bioregion, US 501(c)(3), EIN 85-4402939, Seattle WA. Fiscal-sponsor fee 10% ($15,000), held within the request. Natalia to sanity-check. No em-dashes (per Brandon).
