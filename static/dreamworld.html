The dreamworld can listen to your hands

This page can react to your gestures. Palms together at your heart remembers the past dreams while you hold it. A thumb-to-index pinch gathers dreams by cluster; thumb-to-middle gathers the whole field. A gentle open-palm sweep shifts the field through time, and two hands meeting fingertip-to-fingertip sends a ripple through the dreams.

The detection runs entirely in your browser:

  • No video, no images, no biometric data leaves your computer.
  • Nothing is recorded — every frame is forgotten as the next arrives.
  • You can turn the camera off at any time. The dreamworld still works without it.

A green dot near the top-right shows when the camera is on.
